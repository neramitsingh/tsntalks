Privacy

This site sells sponsorship on a podcast. It has no ads, no tracking and no cookies of its own. Here is everything it touches about you, and what you can ask us to do with it.

In short

  • Reading the site collects nothing we keep. The companies that host it and serve its fonts and pictures see your IP address, as any website's hosts do.
  • Emailing us gives us your address and what you wrote. We use it to reply.
  • Signing in to analytics (Sunny's team only) stores your email address so we can send the link.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Last updated 19 October 2026

01 Who we are

TSN Talks is presented by Thai Sikh News in Bangkok. Thai Sikh News is the data controller for this site under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), the PDPA.

Write to us about anything on this page at thaisikhnews@gmail.com.

02 What we collect, and why

When you read any page
We do not collect anything ourselves: no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no forms. To show you the page, your browser asks other services for it, and they receive your IP address, your browser type and the page you asked for: GitHub, which hosts the site; Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces; and the image servers of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, which serve the post pictures on the live numbers page. The live numbers themselves come from Supabase, our database host. We do not receive these records.
When you email us
Pressing Talk to us or Ask about this opens your own email app. If you send the message, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you wrote. We use it to answer you and to discuss a sponsorship you asked about. Legal basis: steps you asked us to take before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us.
When you sign in to analytics
The analytics page is private to the TSN team. Signing in sends your email address to Supabase, which emails you a one-time link and keeps a record of your account and its sign-ins. Your browser keeps the sign-in in its own storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. We use your address only to let you in and to check it against the list of people allowed to read the numbers. Legal basis: providing the service you asked to use.

03 Who else sees it

Only the services named above, each for its own part: GitHub (hosting), Google (fonts, and Gmail, where our mailbox is), YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (post images) and Supabase (the numbers and the analytics sign-in). They act on our behalf or under their own privacy policies. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

These services store data outside Thailand, including in the United States. Where we send your information abroad, we rely on the protections those providers offer under their own terms, as the PDPA allows.

04 How long we keep it

Emails: as long as we are talking with you, and for up to two years after, unless a sponsorship contract needs us to keep them longer for accounting. Analytics accounts: while you have access, and deleted within 30 days of your access ending or of your asking.

05 Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • send it to you or to someone else in a usable format;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or stop using it for a while;
  • stop using it, if you object to how we use it;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, at any time.

Email thaisikhnews@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you think we have mishandled your data, you can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee at pdpc.or.th.

06 Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
